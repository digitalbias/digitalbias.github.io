The tell

If you disappeared for a month, would the wheels come off?

If the honest answer is yes—or “probably”—that’s not a time problem. It’s a systems problem.

  • Scheduling, exceptions, and “the way we do things” still live in your head
  • You tried to delegate and the work bounced back
  • Every important decision still waits on you
  • Time off isn’t time off, because the phone doesn’t stop

The business can only grow as fast as you can personally carry it. That ceiling is optional.

Why me

I learned this the expensive way.

I spent 23 years in software and operations—building products, leading teams, teaching process. Then I bought a Conserva Irrigation franchise and ran it myself. No team. No safety net. A truck and a book of work I had to invent systems for.

I grew it to 1,500+ homes and kept 95%+ of those customers. I was also wearing every hat: sales, scheduling, service, books. I knew the theory of delegation. Living it with real money on the line is different.

So I wrote the work down. Automated the repetitive parts. Handed off what didn’t need me. The business got better. I could take a week off without my phone lighting up.

That’s the work I now do with other owners.

I speak in systems, stories, and the occasional Star Wars reference. If you get it, we’ll get along just fine.
