After 23 years in software development, I bought a franchise.

That probably sounds strange to people who knew me as a technologist. Why would someone who spent two decades building software products and leading engineering teams decide to buy into a home services franchise?

The honest answer: I wanted to own something. I wanted to test everything I'd spent 20+ years learning about operations, systems, and leadership against something real — a business with customers, weather, equipment, and a payroll that depended on me actually getting it right.

This is Part 1 of a two-part story. In this post, I'll cover how I decided to buy a franchise at all — the reasoning, the alternatives I considered, and what I was wrong about. Part 2 will cover why Conserva specifically, and what the first year actually taught me.
