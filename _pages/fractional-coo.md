## The Problem With Wearing Every Hat

You're the CEO, the COO, the HR department, and the janitor. You handle escalations because nobody else knows how. You're in every important meeting because decisions don't get made without you. You can't take a vacation without your phone buzzing constantly.

This isn't just exhausting—it's a growth ceiling. The business can only scale as fast as you can personally carry it.

The solution isn't working harder. It's building systems.

## What a Fractional COO Does

A fractional COO brings executive-level operations expertise to your business on a part-time basis. You get the strategy and execution capacity of a senior operations leader—without the $150,000+ salary.

For small businesses with 2–20 employees, this is often exactly the right leverage point.

**I don't just advise. I work alongside you** to design, document, and implement the systems that take the operational load off your plate.

## How We Work Together

### Phase 1: Operations Audit (Month 1)
We start by understanding where you are. I map your current workflows, interview you (and key team members if relevant), and identify the top bottlenecks costing you time and money. You get a clear picture of what's working, what isn't, and what to tackle first.

### Phase 2: System Design (Months 2–3)
We build the infrastructure. Standard operating procedures. Delegation frameworks. Communication systems. Hiring and onboarding processes. Metrics and accountability structures. The goal is a business that can run on documented systems, not just tribal knowledge stored in your head.

### Phase 3: Implementation & Refinement (Ongoing)
We implement, test, and refine. Real systems require iteration—especially when you're handing off responsibilities for the first time. I stay involved through the transition so it actually sticks.

## What You'll Walk Away With

- A fully documented operations playbook your team can follow
- Delegation structures so you can stop being in every conversation
- Metrics and KPIs your team owns (not just reports you read)
- Confidence that the business can run without you in the room
- Time back in your week for growth, strategy, and life outside work

## Is This Right for You?

Fractional COO consulting works best when:

- You have a team (even a small one) and need to stop doing everything yourself
- You're generating revenue but feel like you're running on a hamster wheel
- You've tried building systems before but they never quite took hold
- You're serious about investing in your business infrastructure

If you're a solo operator just starting out, career coaching may be a better starting point.
