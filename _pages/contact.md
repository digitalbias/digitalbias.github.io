## Schedule a Free 30-Minute Consultation

You don't need to have everything figured out before we talk. Bring your biggest operational challenge—or just a general sense that something isn't working—and we'll figure out whether I can help.

In 30 minutes, we'll cover:

- **Your biggest operational pain point right now** — what's costing you the most time or energy
- **Where you want the business (or career) to be in 12 months**
- **Whether fractional COO consulting or career coaching is the right fit** — or if something else would serve you better

No high-pressure sales. No commitment required. If I'm not the right fit, I'll tell you.
