## The short version

1. **Sort:** split your pool by color, pull out Spacecraft, and keep your tokens on the table.
2. **Find your power:** pull every bomb and every removal spell first.
3. **Pick two colors:** the deepest pair wins. Hybrid gold cards count for either color.
4. **Build the 40:** 17 lands (16 with two or three landcyclers), 23 spells, 14 to 17 creatures, and seven or eight two-drops.
5. **Play first,** unless your deck is slow and built on card advantage.

And leave Kobayashi Maru Scenario in your sideboard. It says you lose the game.
