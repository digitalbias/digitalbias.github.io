## Writing and Coaching Update
*October 19, 2025*

A lot has happened since my last update, so let me catch you up.

### The Writing Life

I've completed first drafts for two new novels — **The Manticore's Shadow** (a fantasy thriller about an elven soldier defying orders to battle a magician controlling manticores) and **Faultline** (a sci-fi thriller about ex-Ranger Cole McKnight using ley line powers to battle a corporate conspiracy). That brings my total works in progress to seven.

More exciting: I recently received an **Honorable Mention from the Writers of the Future Contest** — a significant milestone for me. I've been writing science fiction and fantasy since high school, and having that recognized externally means a lot. I plan to keep submitting.

Currently in first editorial pass:
- **Rift Walker** — sci-fi/fantasy thriller, 30 of 66 chapters edited
- **Growing in Faith** — gospel-centered nonfiction, revisions underway after editor feedback

**MBA Essentials** — the sketchnote book compressing an MBA into something actually usable — continues to progress. Three semesters remain for editing. [Sign up to get a sample chapter](/mba_notebook/).

### What's Changed on the Site

The site has been updated with the latest on all current projects. If you're following along on the fiction side, the [Writing page](/writing/) has progress bars and current status for everything.
